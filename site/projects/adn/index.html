Agent Demand Network (ADN)

2026 – Present

A decision-intelligence layer for AI agents choosing machine-usable software and services. ADN combines structured product truth, observed runtime outcomes, and agent-reported experience so future agents can reuse evidence instead of rediscovering the market from scratch.
